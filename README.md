# KUAN

A card-collecting game with a built-in focus timer, set in a Bisaya neighbourhood.
Focus to earn tokens, buy packs at the sari-sari store, collect 84 cards, build a deck and fight.

"Kuan" is the Bisaya word for the thing whose name you forgot. In a fight, a Kuan is a wildcard
that becomes a copy of the last card your opponent played.

## Download

Get the latest version from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)**.

| You have | Download | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone | `KUAN-x.y.z.apk` | about 4 MB |
| Windows PC (64-bit) | `KUAN-Setup-x.y.z.exe` | about 120 MB |

There is no iPhone or Mac version yet.

## Installing on Android

1. Download the `.apk` file on your phone and open it.
2. Your phone will ask to allow installing apps from your browser or chat app. Allow it.
3. If Play Protect warns that it does not recognise the app, choose **Install anyway**.
4. Open KUAN from your app drawer.

## Installing on Windows

1. Download the `.exe` file and run it.
2. Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen, because the installer is not signed
   by a big company. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. It adds a desktop shortcut.

## Good to know

- This is an early prototype made for friends. Expect rough edges.
- Your progress is saved on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no accounts or ads.
- To update, download the newer version and install it over the old one. Your progress is kept.
- On Windows, **Game → Export save** makes a backup file you can keep or move to another PC.
- The Windows app has a floating focus timer that stays on top of other windows
  (the **Float** button on the timer screen). On Android you get a notification when a session ends.
